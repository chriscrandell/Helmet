Smart book overview

The expeditionary communications smart book: one place for the 100/200/300-level training material and the pocket-reference content. It's organized so a new Airman can start at 100 and an experienced tech can jump straight to the 300-level equipment pages.

How the levels work

LevelPurposeFormat
100Theory: why it works. Vocabulary, physics, protocols.Concept pages, diagrams, self-check questions
200Operating fundamentals: how to set it up and run it correctly.Procedures, checklists, command references
300Refresh the theory, then apply engineering, implementation and troubleshooting to specific hardware.Equipment pages modeled on the PRC-163 RF course, extendable to other radios and legacy kit

Writing rules for this book

  • Unclassified and releasable only. Nothing from classified or CUI sources, no crypto keying procedures, no unit-specific frequencies or locations. Equipment specifics come from approved, releasable TMs. When in doubt, leave it out and point to the controlled reference.
  • One topic per page. Put the 100-level "why" first, then 200 "how", then 300 "this specific box".
  • Every procedure ends with "how do I know it worked?"
  • Pocket-reference candidates (tables, command lists, checklists) are written so they can be lifted straight into the 160 × 88 mm Word reference.
