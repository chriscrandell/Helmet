Knowledge wiki

Study notes, quick references and the expeditionary comms smart book, organized by category. Pages marked Solid are reliable references, Draft pages are still growing, and greyed-out pages are planned.

Adding to the wiki

  1. Copy Knowledge/_template.html to a new file name, e.g. ospf-deep-dive.html.
  2. Write the content between the marked lines (headings, tables, code blocks and checklists are all styled already).
  3. Register it in Knowledge/wiki-data.js under the right category. It then appears here, in every page's sidebar and in search.
  4. To add a whole new category (e.g. Linux), add a new block to categories in the same file.

Every page also has a My notes box at the bottom for quick capture. It's saved in the browser, so move anything worth keeping into the page itself.
