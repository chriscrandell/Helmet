Weekly rhythm

  • Pick one primary goal per quarter. The others get maintenance time only.
  • Short sessions most days beat one long weekend session, especially for subnetting and CLI fluency.
  • Lab what you read within 48 hours. Write a wiki page or note for anything you had to look up twice.
  • Book the exam date early. A date on the calendar sets the pace.
