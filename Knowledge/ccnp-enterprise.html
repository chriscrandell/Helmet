CCNP Enterprise

Professional-level enterprise networking. Two exams: the ENCOR 350-401 core and one concentration exam of your choice. Passing ENCOR also qualifies you to attempt the CCIE Enterprise lab. Valid for 3 years.

Suggested path

  1. CCNA solid first, especially subnetting, OSPF and STP without thinking.
  2. ENCOR: build a lab topology you keep extending (CML, EVE-NG or GNS3 on the Proxmox host).
  3. ENARSI next: it reuses most of ENCOR's routing, so book it soon after ENCOR while it's fresh.
  4. Automate your own lab with Python: see Python (Netmiko and the ipaddress module).

Study checklist

  • Lab environment built and saved (topology + startup configs)
  • OSPF: area types, LSAs, summarization, path selection
  • EIGRP: named mode, stub, summarization, variance
  • BGP: neighbors, path attributes, best-path selection, route maps
  • Redistribution and loop prevention (tags, filtering)
  • RSTP / MST tuning
  • GRE, IPsec, DMVPN
  • VRF-lite
  • QoS: classification, marking, queuing
  • IP SLA, NetFlow, SPAN, conditional debugging
  • CoPP, 802.1X, AAA with TACACS+/RADIUS
  • NETCONF/RESTCONF + YANG, Python, Ansible
  • ENCOR booked
