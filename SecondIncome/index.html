Before anything earns a dollar, as an active-duty member:

  • Talk to your base legal office's ethics counselor about outside employment and activities under the Joint Ethics Regulation. Prior approval or reporting may be required, and the rules depend on the work.
  • No use of government time, equipment, networks or non-public information, and no use of your rank or position to promote the business.
  • Watch for conflicts of interest with your duties. That matters especially for anything comms, IT or acquisition-adjacent, given your role.
  • Plan for taxes. Self-employment income usually means quarterly estimated payments. A tax professional (or your installation's tax center in season) can confirm what applies to you.
