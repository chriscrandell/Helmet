Design doc

PitchOne or two sentences: what the player does and why it's fun.
Core loopWhat the player does minute-to-minute (e.g. explore → find → solve → reward).
Player & goalWho you play as and what winning looks like.
The "code" mechanicHow code / hunting shows up in play: puzzles, collectibles, programming the character, something else?
AudienceWho it's for, including the skill level it assumes.
Platform & engineBrowser, desktop or handheld, and the engine or language.
Art & audio styleReference games or a mood board link.
Scope for v1The smallest version worth playing: levels, mechanics, length.
Out of scope (for now)Ideas parked on purpose.

Milestones

  • Concept locked: pitch, core loop and v1 scope written above
  • Paper / greybox prototype: the core loop is playable in its ugliest form
  • Fun check: someone else plays it and you watch without helping
  • Vertical slice: one level at close to final quality
  • Content complete: all v1 levels and mechanics in
  • Polish & bug pass: menus, save/load, audio, settings
  • Release: published wherever it's going
